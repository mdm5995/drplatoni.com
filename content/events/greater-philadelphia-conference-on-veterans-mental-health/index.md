## About the Event

**Council for Relationships — Operation Home and Healing** presents *The Greater Philadelphia Conference on Veterans Mental Health: Making Mental Health a Priority, Strategies and Treatment for Veterans and Military Families.*

This full-day training conference features the nation's leading experts in military behavioral health. Mental health professionals will learn common gaps and misconceptions in the treatment of Veterans and military families, alternative treatment techniques for PTSD, suicide risk and prevention, and the realities of reintegration into civilian life. Veterans, military families, staff of Veterans Service Organizations, and advocates are all welcome.

**Conference Highlights:**
- Nationally recognized Veterans behavioral health experts
- Training for mental health professionals working with Veterans and military families
- Interactive Group Discussions
- Networking Opportunities
- **6 CEUs available**
