## Event Overview
Law enforcement officers, elected officials, public dignitaries, family survivors, and the public are invited to gather on **Monday, September 27, 2021**, to honor the **40 Montgomery County law enforcement officers** who have made the ultimate sacrifice over the past 131 years[cite: 1].

---

## Ceremony Schedule
*   **10:00 AM:** Procession assembly begins at the Temple Israel parking lot[cite: 1].
*   **10:15 AM:** Procession of officers, retirees, and cruisers departs[cite: 1].
*   **10:45 AM:** Procession arrives at the ceremony site at **Riverscape**[cite: 1].
*   **11:00 AM:** Memorial ceremony begins[cite: 1].

---

## Featured Speakers
### Keynote Speaker: Dr. Kathy Platoni, Psy. D., DAIPM, FAIS
Dr. Platoni is a well-respected psychologist specializing in working with officers after traumatic incidents[cite: 1]. She is also a retired Colonel with the **US Army**[cite: 1].

### Survivor Speaker: Denise Dulle
Denise Dulle is the mother of **Sgt. Brian S. Dulle** of the Warren County Sheriff's Office[cite: 1]. Sgt. Dulle, a 12-year veteran and US Army veteran, was tragically killed in the line of duty on May 10, 2011[cite: 1].

## Logistics and Parking
### Public and Official Parking
*   **Location:** Free parking is available at **811 East Monument Street** (east of Meigs Street and Tech Town)[cite: 1].
*   **Shuttle Service:** Available to and from the parking lot to the procession staging area and the ceremony location[cite: 1].
*   **Security:** Volunteers will monitor the lot during the ceremony to ensure vehicle safety[cite: 1].

### Procession Assembly (Officers & Retirees)
*   **Location:** Temple Israel, 130 Riverside Drive[cite: 1].
*   **Constraint:** Due to limited space, each agency is requested to bring **only one** marked cruiser[cite: 1]. No extra parking is available at Temple Israel for personal vehicles[cite: 1].

### Post-Ceremony Luncheon
A complimentary luncheon for law enforcement officers, retirees, and family survivors will follow the ceremony at the **Antioch Shrine** (120 East First Street)[cite: 1]. This meal is provided courtesy of the **Kettering Health Network**, with shuttle service provided to and from the parking area[cite: 1].
