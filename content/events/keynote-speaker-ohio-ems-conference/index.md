## Conference Overview
The **Ohio EMS Conference**, presented by OhioHealth, is a comprehensive educational event designed for emergency medical responders, EMTs, paramedics, nurses, and physicians. The 2026 conference features a hybrid format with an in-person day at the Hilton Columbus/Polaris followed by a live online session via Microsoft Teams.

---

## Keynote Speaker
### Marianne Gausche-Hill, MD, FACEP, FAAP, FAEMS
Dr. Gausche-Hill is the Executive Director of Academic and Clinical Affairs at the Lundquist Institute and a Professor of Clinical Emergency Medicine and Pediatrics at UCLA. She is an internationally recognized leader in EMS and pediatric emergency medicine, best known for her landmark research in pre-hospital airway management and the National Pediatric Readiness Project.

---

## Awards and Ceremonies
The conference serves as a platform to recognize excellence within the Ohio EMS community through several prestigious awards:

*   **The John P. Moore Memorial EMS Award for Excellence:** Bestowed annually upon an Ohio EMS provider who demonstrates outstanding leadership, mentorship, and advocacy in the spirit of the late John P. Moore.
*   **Excellence in EMS Medical Direction Award:** Recognizes an Ohio EMS Physician Medical Director for their critical role in clinical quality, patient safety, and educator leadership.
*   **Excellence in EMS Education Award:** Presented to a faculty or staff member to recognize their significant contributions to EMS training programs.
*   **Alumni Luncheon:** A private gathering for graduates and students of the Franklin County Firefighters/OhioHealth Grant Medical Center EMS Education programs.

---

## Continuing Education
OhioHealth Emergency Medical Services is an approved Continuing Education Provider by the **State of Ohio Department of Public Safety, Division of EMS** (Approval #2084). 
*   **Nursing Credits:** The Ohio Board of Nursing accepts CE activities approved by the Ohio Division of EMS.
*   **Scope:** Programs are intended to enhance clinical knowledge in areas such as trauma, stroke, and STEMI care.
