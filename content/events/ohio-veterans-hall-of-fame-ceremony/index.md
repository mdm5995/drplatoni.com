## 2019 Induction Ceremony
The Ohio Department of Veterans Services (ODVS) is proud to honor 20 outstanding inductees at the **28th annual Induction Ceremony** on **Thursday, November 7, 2019**. These individuals are being recognized not for their time in uniform, but for their significant accomplishments and community impact in their post-military lives. 

The general public and Ohio veterans are encouraged to attend the ceremony to celebrate this stellar class, which represents 13 Ohio counties and four military branches.

## About the Ohio Veterans Hall of Fame
The Ohio Veterans Hall of Fame was established in 1992 by former **Governor George Voinovich** to recognize the post-military achievements of outstanding Ohio veterans. 

*   **Legacy:** Past honorees include astronauts, community volunteers, safety officers, and government officials. Charter members included six U.S. Presidents from Ohio and various Medal of Honor recipients.
*   **Permanent Display:** The members of the Hall of Fame are honored in a permanent display near the Ohio Statehouse at the **Riffe Center** (77 South High Street, Columbus, Ohio).
*   **Administration:** The Hall is administered by the ODVS in collaboration with an Executive Committee comprised of 13 veteran volunteers who serve as advisors and selectors.
