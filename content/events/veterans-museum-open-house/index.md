## Event Overview
The **Veteran Memorial Museum** is hosting an Open House to celebrate its 10-year anniversary on Saturday, May 18, coinciding with Armed Forces Day. The event serves as a fundraiser for the **USA Patriots** (formerly the Wounded Warrior Amputee Softball Team) for their upcoming appearance at Wright State University.

---

## Featured Presentations
Starting at **1:00 PM**, the museum's theater will host three specialized presentations. Please note that seating is limited to the first **100 attendees**, and a **$5 donation** is requested.

### 1. "The Fall League" Documentary & Guest Speakers
*   **Film:** A screening of the award-winning documentary focusing on senior softball, the WWAST, and the camaraderie built through military service and sports.
*   **Speakers:** 
    *   **Tim Gebard:** Documentary producer and Waynesville resident.
    *   **Dr. Kathy Platoni, COL (RET):** A nationally known veterans advocate, author, and editor of *Combat Stress E-Magazine*. Dr. Platoni served in multiple operations including Desert Storm, Iraqi Freedom, and Enduring Freedom.

### 2. Reflections on Freedom
*   **Presenter:** Mr. Al Bailey, President of **Honor Flight-Dayton**.
*   **Content:** A video featuring Dr. Henry Chong reflecting on his childhood in South Korea and the significance of American forces protecting his family.

### 3. USA Patriots (WWAST) Local Impact
*   **Details:** Information regarding the team's 4th year appearing in the Miami Valley. 
*   **Beneficiaries:** Funds raised support local organizations including Fisher Houses (WPAFB & Dayton VA), Honor Flight-Dayton, Combat Outpost Robinson, and the USA Patriots Kid’s Camp.

---

## The Veteran Voices Project
Representatives from **Wright State University’s Veteran & Military Center** will be on-site throughout the day. They are seeking veterans willing to record and share their service stories for the **Veteran Voices Project**, an oral history program affiliated with the Library of Congress.

---

## Museum Information
The Veterans Memorial Museum Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit institution dedicated to the educational memorialization of US Armed Forces Veterans.
