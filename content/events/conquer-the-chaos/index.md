## About the Event

**Heroic Deeds** presents *Conquer the Chaos* — a Responders in Faith Conference dedicated to celebrating, honoring, and connecting First Responders in Faith. This two-day event is open to traditional First Responders and their significant others to attend together.

Sessions will address the **physical, psychological, and faith fitness** of first responders through worship, general sessions with special speakers, hands-on training, and breakout sessions — all with a Christ-centered focus.

---

## Our Awesome Speakers

### Matt Domyancic — *The Tactical Chaplain*
Former Police Officer, Strength Coach, and Chaplain. Matt brings a unique perspective combining law enforcement experience with faith-based ministry and physical wellness.

### Jeff & Denise Naber
Specialists in First Responder Marriage Survival Training and former First Responders themselves. Jeff and Denise offer powerful insight into sustaining healthy relationships under the pressures of first responder life.

### Charlie McMahan
Lead Pastor of Southbrook Church and the host pastor for this event, Charlie brings spiritual leadership and a heart for the first responder community.

### Dr. Kathy Platoni, Psy.D.
Clinical Psychologist and PTSD & Trauma Expert. Dr. Platoni provides evidence-based insight into the psychological toll of first responder work, with practical tools for healing and resilience.

### Comedian Vinnie Montez
Police officer and stand-up comedian. Vinnie brings much-needed laughter and levity to the conference experience.
