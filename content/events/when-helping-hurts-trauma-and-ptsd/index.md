## Sunday Super Session Overview
As part of the **Ohio NENA/APCO Joint Conference**, Dr. Kathy Platoni will lead an intensive session titled **"When Helping Hurts ~ The Silent Epidemic of Trauma and PTSD in the First Responder Community"** on April 14, 2019. This session focuses on the unique psychological challenges faced by those on the front lines of emergency services.

---

## About the Speaker: Dr. Kathy Platoni
Dr. Kathy Platoni, Psy.D., is a practicing clinical psychologist with over 37 years of experience, maintaining a private practice in Centerville, Ohio. Her extensive background includes:

*   **Military Service:** A retired Colonel (2013) from the US Army and current Colonel in the Ohio Military Reserve/State Defense Forces. She has deployed four times in service of her country.
*   **Combat Veteran:** Served in Operation Desert Shield, Operation Iraqi Freedom, and Operation Enduring Freedom.
*   **Specialized Roles:** Serves as the **Dayton SWAT psychologist** and Mental Health Advisor to the Dayton Hostage Negotiation Team.
*   **Advocacy:** A survivor of the 2009 Ft. Hood Massacre, she is an ardent activist for the recognition of the incident as an act of terrorism and was instrumental in securing Purple Heart Medals for the wounded and deceased.
*   **Authorship:** Co-author and editor of landmark books on war trauma, including *Expanding the Circle of Healing~Trauma in Its Wake* and *Healing War Trauma*. She also serves as the Editor for *Combat Stress E-Magazine*.
